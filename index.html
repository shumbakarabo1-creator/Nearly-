Nearly V1

Nearly is an everyday-life discovery app that turns natural requests into useful nearby services and shops.

  • Base Province: Mpumalanga, South Africa
  • Key Hubs: Mbombela, eMalahleni, Middelburg, Secunda, White River, Barberton, Hazyview, Ermelo, Standerton, Lydenburg
  • Navigation: Integrated Google Maps (Turn-by-turn driving, walking, transit)
  • Storage: LocalStorage & SQLite Room
